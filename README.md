# Melanoma_anti-PD1_stromal_MHC1_RNAseq-analysis
Paired pre- vs on-treatment RNA-seq analysis of public melanoma anti-PD-1 data (GSE91061): stromal, MHC-I and cytolytic programmes.
Immune activation: PDCD1, CXCL9, GZMH, PRF1 and ACOD1 rise on treatment (FDR below 0.05).
Cytolytic and MHC-I scores: both increase (FDR 0.0003 and 0.0035) and hold up after excluding outlier samples.
Fibroblast and stromal scores: unchanged in bulk tissue (FDR 0.81 and 0.18).
GRN (progranulin): rises on treatment, more in responders, but this is exploratory (FDR 0.061 and 0.071).
Baseline responders vs non-responders: no significant difference.
